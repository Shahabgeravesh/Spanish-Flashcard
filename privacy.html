Privacy Policy

Spanish Deck · Last updated July 26, 2026

Spanish Deck (“the App”) helps you practice Spanish with flashcards, stories, and exams. This policy explains what information the App handles.

Information we collect

The App does not require an account and does not collect personal information such as your name, email, or phone number.

Learning progress (card mastery, streaks, story completion, and exam scores) is stored only on your device using on-device storage. We do not receive or sync this data to our servers.

Speech

Optional “Listen” features use your device’s built-in text-to-speech system (Apple’s speech APIs). Audio is generated on device and is not uploaded by the App.

Analytics & tracking

The App does not include third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking across other companies’ apps or websites.

Internet access

Core study content ships with the App and works offline. The App may open an external link (for example, to this privacy policy or the free companion website) only when you choose to open it.

Children

The App is rated for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy when the App’s practices change. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.
